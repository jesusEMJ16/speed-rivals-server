Privacy Policy — Speed Rivals

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This policy explains how the Speed Rivals app handles your information. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information Collected

  • Game data (local): your progress, coins, records and chosen pilot nickname are stored on your device.
  • Multiplayer: for online rankings and races, your nickname (chosen by you, not your real name) and match stats (distance, points) are sent to our server. The server assigns a guest ID and stores an authentication hash; your device stores the secret token used to recover that profile. Your nickname, public ID, CP, wins, races and best distance appear in the leaderboard. Avoid using your real name as a nickname if you do not want it published.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): the app shows ads via Google AdMob, which may collect the device advertising identifier, IP address and usage data to serve and measure ads.

2. Use of Information

Information is used only to run the game and online mode, maintain the leaderboard, and display ads that keep the app free.

3. Third Parties

The app uses Google AdMob for advertising. Google's handling of data is governed by its policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can manage ad personalization in your device settings (Ads / Advertising ID).

4. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

5. Security & Retention

Game data is stored locally on your device; you can delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing its data. Online profiles and match results are retained to display standings and prevent the same match from awarding points twice. Clearing app data does not automatically delete the server profile and may remove your access to it. Contact the developer with questions about your data.

For questions about this policy, contact: jesusEMJ@gmail.com
